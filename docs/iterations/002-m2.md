## Deviations from v1

1. $\lambda = 0$ runs only with routing exempt.
2. The dual target $q^{\ast}$ is fixed at 0.75 of the initial mean $J_{route}$ ($\eta_\mu = 2$), not learned.
3. The eligibility uses the frame's own $\pi$ ($\hat{\pi}$ in spiking).
4. If no action neuron spikes, the action is chosen uniformly and counted.

## Problems for `snn-expert`, open

- **Task-term $d_j$ ratchet in spiking.** $d_{max}$ goes from 1.0 to 2.2 within 5 episodes at $\eta = 10$, but stays at 1.03 with $\eta = 0$. The noisy covariance $\kappa$ times $\delta h$, with $h \approx 7$, is rectified at $w_{min}$. The $\epsilon$ term sits only inside $\lambda$, where it is too weak. Options: apply $\epsilon (d_j - 1)$ to the task term as well, or low-pass $\kappa$ before it enters the eligibility.
- **The race reads the early transient.** The first action spike arrives at step 2–3, so it reflects direct C→A routing rather than the stationary $p_a$. The KL grows as the network modularises. A burn-in before the race may be needed.
- **$\lambda > 0$ seals the latent and action modules almost completely** (persistence 0.996), and $p_{\mathcal{A}}$ falls. Check whether this costs return.
